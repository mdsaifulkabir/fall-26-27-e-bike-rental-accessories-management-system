# E-Bike Rental & Accessories Management System
The E-Bike Rental & Accessories Management System is designed for people who want to rent electric bikes and purchase bike accessories, as well as station managers and sellers who need to manage their services and products. The project aims to make the bike rental and accessories purchasing process easier and more organized. The system addresses problems such as difficulty finding available bikes, managing rental information, checking bike availability at different stations, and keeping track of accessory products and customer orders. The prototype includes a Home Page, Login Page, Registration Page, and separate dashboards for Admin, Customer, Station Manager, and Seller. The forms include user registration, login, bike booking, ride information, fare calculation, station information, bike status updates, product listing, stock updates, and customer orders.
